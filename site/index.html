02 / THE DECISION LAYER

Your application.
Your options.
Your Jev.

Route a ticket, select a tool, or choose a robot’s next action. Use the same interface across your applications.

  1. 01

    Bring a model

    Start with a released checkpoint or a supported open model.

  2. 02

    Train on your decisions

    Use your labelled data with SFT. Explore experimental RLCR for further training.

  3. 03

    Make the call

    Get a choice and its probabilities. Set your own threshold for human review.

Meet the API ↗
YOUR APPLICATIONPython
from jevany import Choice, JevClient

jev = JevClient("http://127.0.0.1:8008")
result = jev.system_one(
    state={"ticket": "I was charged twice."},
    questions={
        "department": Choice(
            instructions="Which team?",
            criteria={
                "billing": "Payment problems",
                "shipping": "Delivery problems",
            },
        ),
    },
)
